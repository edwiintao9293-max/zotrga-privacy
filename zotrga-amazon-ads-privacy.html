Privacy Notice

Effective date: September 12, 2026

ZOTRGA operates an internal Amazon Ads Reporting Tool solely for managing and analyzing advertising performance for ZOTRGA's own Amazon advertising accounts.

Data we access

The tool accesses Amazon Ads account, campaign, and performance-reporting data, including impressions, clicks, advertising cost, attributed sales, and reporting metadata. The tool is not intended to collect buyer names, email addresses, addresses, payment information, or other buyer personal information.

How we use data

We use this data only for internal advertising-performance reporting and business analysis. The tool does not create, edit, pause, or optimize campaigns, bids, budgets, keywords, or targeting.

Data sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share Amazon Ads data with third parties, except where required by law.

Data security and retention

API credentials are stored securely and access is limited to authorized personnel. Advertising data is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary for internal reporting and accounting purposes.
